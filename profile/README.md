# 53ON

**AI with a memory of its own, on your own PC.**

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**53ON Harness**](https://github.com/53on/53on-harness) | The open-source desktop shell (MIT) that gives your AI a memory, and hands, eyes and voice on your Windows PC. For Claude, GPT, Gemini, Qwen and local AI. Works with Claude Code. |
| [**MemoriaBios**](https://github.com/53on/memoriabios) | The memory: it chooses what to keep, forgets on a curve, sleeps, and knows how much it knows. No extra model calls. |
| [**53ON AI Server**](https://github.com/53on/53on-ai-server) | Your own OpenAI-compatible AI server for AMD Ryzen AI Max: local models, client keys and quotas (MIT). |
